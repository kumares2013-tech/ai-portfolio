Which card pays you the most for how you already spend?

Type in a normal month. You get your three best cards, what each one would actually give you in dollars, and every catch in plain English.

Four pain points in choosing a card today, and what this prototype fixes

Pain 1

80+ cards, and no answer for you

Comparison sites list every card and let you sort them. None of them tells you which card is best for the way you spend.

First-time applicants post their monthly spending line by line on r/singaporefi and ask strangers which card to get.
Fixed

Type your month in once. Every card is checked against it. You get your top three, and one line on why each of the other cards lost.

Pain 2

Rewards written in code

Rewards are shown as "S$1 = 20X Points (8 miles)… for cardholders who maintain at least S$50,000 average daily balance". A first-timer can't turn that into money.

Fixed

Dollars a month, and what you actually keep in year one after the annual fee. Miles are converted at a value you choose.

Pain 3

The catch is in the fine print

Minimum spends and caps decide whether a card pays you, and they sit in the terms. Miss an $800 minimum by $1 and a "6%" card pays 0.25%.

Fixed

Every minimum and cap is checked against your numbers, in plain English: "You'd hit the cap, so $69 of it is lost."

Pain 4

The sign-up gift never arrives

Gifts need a spend target by a deadline, the bank's reference number and a separate claim form. People find out too late.

"it still says 'Pending Gift Qualification'", from a user on r/singaporefi (Aug 2026) who had already met the spend.
Fixed

A dated plan worked out from your own spending pace: the reference number to keep, the claim step, the day you'll hit the target and the deadline. One tap adds them to your calendar.

      The words, in plain English

      Minimum spend
      The card only pays its good rate if you put at least this much on it in a month. Miss it by $1 and you usually get almost nothing.
      Cap
      The most the card will pay you each month, however much you spend. Spending past the cap earns you nothing extra.
      Miles per dollar (mpd)
      How many air miles you get for each S$1. 4 mpd on $500 is 2,000 miles.
      Annual fee waiver
      Most banks waive the fee in year one. After that you often have to call and ask. It is usually granted, but it is not promised.
      Sign-up gift
      Left out of the numbers above on purpose. Gifts change every few weeks and come with their own conditions.

      Why this exists

      Comparison sites ask what you spend on. Almost none ask how much. Without the amounts, a site can't tell you whether you'd clear a minimum spend or blow through a cap, which is exactly what decides whether a card is any good for you. So people end up posting their monthly spending on Reddit and asking strangers which card to get.

      This page does that sum for them, and shows the working.
